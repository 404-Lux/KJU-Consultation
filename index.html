Kenny Johnson: When you notice an unfamiliar collection item on your Canadian credit report, the most common advice is often the most damaging: either panic and pay an unverified collection agency, or send generic copy-paste dispute letters downloaded from the internet.

In Canada, under provincial consumer protection and credit reporting acts, debt assignees and furnisher institutions must adhere to strict verification standards. Before you take any action, you need to understand whether the account is reporting accurately across both Equifax Canada and TransUnion Canada, what documented proof exists, and what your rights are.
