01. Commitment to Canadian Privacy Standards

Kenny Johnson University ("KJU", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Quebec’s Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector (Law 25), and applicable provincial consumer reporting regulations.

02. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that is voluntarily provided through our qualification questionnaire and booking funnel:

  • Contact details (Full name, email address, Canadian telephone number).
  • Province or territory of residence.
  • Self-reported high-level credit file characteristics (e.g. presence of collection tradelines, discharged insolvency status).
  • Technical attribution parameters (such as campaign source or Meta ad parameters) for internal analytics.
IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE

We do NOT collect, store, or ask for your Social Insurance Number (SIN), banking passwords, online banking credentials, Credit Karma login credentials, or Equifax login credentials through this website.

03. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used exclusively to:

  • Determine your eligibility for KJU’s diagnostic credit education consultation.
  • Coordinate and schedule your 1-on-1 strategy call with our credit specialists.
  • Provide educational materials and follow-up resources relevant to Canadian credit reporting.
  • Fulfill our legal and professional record-keeping requirements.

04. Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Protection

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal data to third-party lenders, debt relief aggregators, or unauthorized third-party marketing companies.
